The last two seconds before a crash.
We measure them, model them, and design for them.

Crash databases record the injury but never the reaction. Our group works in that gap: real human behavior under threat, digital pedestrians that behave the same way, and vehicles that act before impact.

Pedestrian behavior & pedestrian–vehicle interaction

What do people actually do when a car is about to hit them?

0.17 s
decision time of pedestrians who avoid the crash, versus 0.41 s for those who are hit
  • Immersive VR conflicts with motion capture and EEG, not roadside cameras
  • Safety envelope, three decision modes, and why uncertainty peaks under high urgency

  • Digital pedestrian in CARLA, validated on real near-crash trajectories

AI-driven integrated safety & injury prevention

When the risk is visible, how should braking, steering and protection act together?

−59%
probability of severe head injury when the integrated system triggers at TTC −0.5 s
  • Prediction-based active hood: 200 ms earlier, up to 62% HIC reduction, zero false positives on 110 real near-misses
